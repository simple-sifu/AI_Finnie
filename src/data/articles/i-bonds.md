# I Bonds

I bonds are U.S. savings bonds designed to protect your money from inflation.

## How they work

- The interest rate combines a **fixed rate** (set when you buy) and an **inflation rate** that resets every six months.
- When inflation is high, I bond interest goes up.
- Interest is added to the bond's value; you receive it when you cash the bond in.

## Rules to know

- Buy them at TreasuryDirect.gov.
- You must hold them for at least **one year** before cashing in.
- If you cash in before **five years**, you lose the last three months of interest.
- There's a yearly purchase limit per person, so check TreasuryDirect for the current amount.

## Taxes

Interest is subject to federal income tax but exempt from state and local income taxes. You can usually wait to pay federal tax until you cash the bond in.

## Who they suit

I bonds can be a safe place for money you won't need for at least a year and want protected from inflation. They aren't a replacement for long-term stock investing.
