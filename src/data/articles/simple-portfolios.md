# Building a Simple Portfolio

You don't need dozens of investments to build a solid portfolio. Many beginners use just one to three broad, low-cost funds.

## Option 1: One fund

A **target date fund** or a balanced fund holds stocks and bonds in one investment and rebalances for you. It's the simplest approach.

## Option 2: A few index funds

A widely used approach combines three broad index funds:

1. a **U.S. total stock market** fund,
2. an **international stock** fund,
3. a **U.S. total bond market** fund.

You choose the percentage in each based on your goals and risk tolerance, then rebalance now and then.

## What makes a portfolio "good"

- **Diversified:** spread across many companies and asset types.
- **Low cost:** low expense ratios leave more for you.
- **Matches your goals:** the right mix for your time horizon and risk tolerance.
- **Easy to stick with:** a plan you'll follow through ups and downs.

## Keep in mind

Simple doesn't mean risk-free. Any portfolio with stocks will go down at times.
