# What Is Investing?

Investing means putting money into something you expect to grow in value over time, such as stocks, bonds or funds. Saving means setting money aside somewhere safe, like a bank savings account, so it's there when you need it.

## Why people invest

Money in a savings account is safe, but it usually grows slowly. Over many years, rising prices (inflation) can eat away at what that money can buy. Investing gives your money a chance to grow faster than inflation, which is why it's the usual way people build wealth for long-term goals like retirement.

## The trade-off

Investments can lose value. A stock you buy for $100 could be worth $80 next month. In general, investments that can grow more also carry more risk of losing money in the short run. That's why many people keep short-term money in savings and invest money they won't need for years.

## Getting started

Before investing, it helps to have an emergency fund and a plan for paying down high-interest debt. Then decide what you're investing for and when you'll need the money. Those two answers shape almost every other choice.
