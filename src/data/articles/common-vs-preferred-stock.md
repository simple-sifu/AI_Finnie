# Common vs. Preferred Stock

Companies can issue two main types of stock.

## Common stock

This is what most people own.

- **Voting rights:** you usually get to vote on things like electing the board of directors.
- **Dividends:** may be paid, but aren't guaranteed and can change.
- **Growth:** you share fully in the company's success through rising share prices.
- **Last in line:** if the company goes bankrupt, common shareholders are paid after everyone else, and often get nothing.

## Preferred stock

A mix between a stock and a bond.

- **Fixed dividends:** usually a set dividend, paid before common shareholders get any.
- **Usually no voting rights.**
- **Less growth:** prices tend to move less with the company's success.
- **Higher priority:** paid before common shareholders if the company is liquidated, but after bondholders.

## Which do beginners own?

Most beginners own common stock, usually through funds. Preferred stock is more often used by investors who want steady income.
