# Staying Calm When Markets Drop

Market drops are a normal part of investing. How you react can matter as much as what you own.

## Drops happen regularly

Stock markets have gone through many declines of 10%, 20% or more, and have historically recovered over time, though past results don't guarantee the future and recoveries can take years.

## Common mistakes

- **Panic selling:** selling after a big drop locks in losses and can mean missing the recovery.
- **Chasing what's hot:** buying something because it just went up a lot.
- **Checking too often:** daily price watching makes normal swings feel scarier.

## What can help

- **Have a plan:** know your goals, time horizon and target mix before markets get rough.
- **Keep an emergency fund:** so you're not forced to sell investments to cover surprises.
- **Keep investing regularly:** steady contributions buy more shares when prices are low.
- **Remember your time horizon:** money you won't need for 20 years doesn't need to be rescued today.

## When to rethink

If a drop makes you realize you can't handle the risk, it may be worth adjusting your mix calmly, not in a panic.
