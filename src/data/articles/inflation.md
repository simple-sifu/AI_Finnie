# Inflation and Your Money

Inflation is the general rise in prices over time. When prices go up, each dollar buys a little less.

## Why it matters for savers

If your savings earn 1% a year but prices rise 3% a year, your money is losing about 2% of its buying power each year. You have more dollars, but they buy less. This is called **inflation risk**.

## How inflation adds up

At 3% inflation, something that costs $100 today costs about:

- $134 in 10 years
- $181 in 20 years
- $243 in 30 years

## Protecting against inflation

- **Long-term investing:** over long periods, stocks have historically grown faster than inflation, though with ups and downs along the way.
- **Inflation-protected bonds:** U.S. Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS) and I bonds adjust for inflation.
- **Keep short-term money in savings anyway:** inflation is a long-term problem. For money you need soon, safety matters more.
