# Types of Investment Accounts

Before you can invest, you need an account to hold your investments. The type of account affects taxes and when you can take money out.

## Brokerage account (taxable)

A regular investing account at a brokerage firm. You can put in and take out money anytime. You pay taxes on dividends, interest and gains along the way.

## Retirement accounts

These offer tax benefits in exchange for rules about withdrawals:

- **401(k) or 403(b):** through your employer, often with a matching contribution.
- **Traditional IRA:** contributions may be tax-deductible; withdrawals in retirement are taxed.
- **Roth IRA:** no tax break now, but qualified withdrawals in retirement are tax-free.

## Other accounts

- **Health savings account (HSA):** for medical costs, if you have a qualifying high-deductible health plan.
- **529 plan:** for education savings.

## Which comes first?

Many people start with their employer's 401(k), at least up to the full match, then consider an IRA, then a taxable brokerage account.
