# Diversification

Diversification means spreading your money across many different investments so that one bad investment doesn't sink your whole portfolio. It's the idea behind "don't put all your eggs in one basket."

## Ways to diversify

- **Across companies:** own many stocks instead of a few.
- **Across asset types:** mix stocks, bonds and cash.
- **Across industries:** technology, health care, energy, consumer goods and more.
- **Across regions:** U.S. and international companies.

## The easy way

Broad index funds and target date funds give you instant diversification in a single investment, holding hundreds or thousands of companies.

## What diversification can and can't do

- **It can** reduce the damage from any single company or industry having problems.
- **It can't** protect you from losses when the whole market falls, and it doesn't guarantee a profit.

## How Finnie measures it

Finnie gives your portfolio a diversification score from 0 to 100. A single stock scores 0; a portfolio spread across several funds scores high, because each fund already holds many companies.
