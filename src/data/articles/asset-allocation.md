# Asset Allocation

Asset allocation is how you divide your money among different types of investments, mainly **stocks**, **bonds** and **cash**.

## Why it matters

Each type behaves differently:

- **Stocks:** highest growth potential, biggest ups and downs.
- **Bonds:** steadier, with income, but lower growth.
- **Cash:** safest and most available, but may not keep up with inflation.

Your mix has a big effect on both your long-term returns and how bumpy the ride feels.

## What shapes your allocation

- **Time horizon:** more time usually means you can hold more stocks.
- **Risk tolerance:** how much ups and downs you can handle without panicking.
- **Goals:** money for a house in 3 years and retirement in 30 years need different mixes.

## Example mixes

- **Aggressive:** 90% stocks, 10% bonds
- **Moderate:** 60% stocks, 40% bonds
- **Conservative:** 30% stocks, 60% bonds, 10% cash

These are illustrations, not recommendations.

## Keeping it on track

As markets move, your mix drifts. Rebalancing brings it back to your target.
