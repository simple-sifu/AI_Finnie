# Risk Tolerance

Risk tolerance is how much ups and downs in your investments you're willing and able to handle.

## Two sides of risk tolerance

- **Ability to take risk:** your situation. A long time horizon, a stable income and an emergency fund all increase how much risk you can afford.
- **Willingness to take risk:** your comfort level. Would a 25% drop make you lose sleep or sell in a panic?

Both matter. A good plan fits the lower of the two.

## A simple self-check

Imagine your $10,000 investment drops to $7,500 in a few months. Would you:

- **Sell** to stop the losses? → more **conservative**
- **Hold** and wait it out? → **moderate**
- **Buy more** while prices are low? → more **aggressive**

## Why it matters

Investors who take on more risk than they can stomach often sell at the worst time, locking in losses.

## How Finnie uses it

You can set your risk tolerance (Conservative, Moderate or Aggressive) in Finnie's sidebar. Goal planning uses it to choose an assumed return, and portfolio analysis compares it with your portfolio's risk level.
