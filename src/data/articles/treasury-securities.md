# U.S. Treasury Securities

Treasury securities are loans to the U.S. government. They are backed by the full faith and credit of the United States, which makes them among the safest investments for getting your money back.

## Main types

- **Treasury bills (T-bills):** short-term, maturing in one year or less. Sold at a discount; you receive the full face value at maturity.
- **Treasury notes:** mature in 2 to 10 years and pay interest every six months.
- **Treasury bonds:** mature in 20 or 30 years and pay interest every six months.
- **TIPS (Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities):** their principal adjusts with inflation.

## How to buy

You can buy Treasuries directly from the government at TreasuryDirect.gov, or through a brokerage account. Many funds also hold Treasuries.

## Tax benefit

Interest on Treasuries is subject to federal income tax but exempt from state and local income taxes.

## Keep in mind

Treasuries are safe from default, but their prices still move with interest rates if you sell before maturity.
