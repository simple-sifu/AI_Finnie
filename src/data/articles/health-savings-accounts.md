# Health Savings Accounts (HSAs)

A health savings account (HSA) is a tax-advantaged account for medical expenses. You can open one only if you're covered by a qualifying **high-deductible health plan** (HDHP).

## Triple tax advantage

1. **Contributions** are tax-deductible (or pre-tax through your paycheck).
2. **Growth** is tax-free while the money stays in the account.
3. **Withdrawals** for qualified medical expenses are tax-free.

## Other features

- The money is yours to keep, even if you change jobs or health plans.
- Unused money rolls over every year; there's no "use it or lose it."
- Many HSAs let you invest the balance in funds.
- The IRS sets a yearly contribution limit.

## Non-medical withdrawals

- **Before age 65:** taxed as income, plus a 20% additional tax.
- **After age 65:** taxed as income, with no additional tax, much like a traditional IRA.

## Why some people invest their HSA

Some people pay current medical bills out of pocket and let their HSA grow as an extra retirement account for future health costs.
