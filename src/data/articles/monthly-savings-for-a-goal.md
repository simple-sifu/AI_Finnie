# How Much to Save Each Month for a Goal

To reach a savings goal, you need to know how much to set aside each month. Three things decide the number: **how much you need**, **how long you have** and **how fast your money grows**.

## Without growth

Divide the goal by the number of months.

**Example:** $20,000 in 5 years (60 months) → $20,000 ÷ 60 = about **$333 a month**.

## With growth

If your savings earn a return, you need to put in less, because growth does part of the work.

**Example:** the same $20,000 in 5 years, earning about 4% a year → about **$302 a month**.

Over longer periods, the difference grows. Over 30 years, growth can do much more of the work than your contributions.

## Money you already have counts

If you've already saved part of the goal, that amount grows too, lowering what you need to add each month.

## Be realistic about growth

Higher returns usually mean more risk. For short-term goals, assume little or no growth. For long-term goals, use a moderate assumption, not the best case.
