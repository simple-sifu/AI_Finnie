# Saving for Short-Term Goals

A short-term goal is something you'll need money for within a few years, like a car, a vacation or a home down payment.

## Why safety comes first

With a short time horizon, there's little time to recover if investments fall. If the stock market drops 20% right before you need your down payment, you may have to delay your plans.

## Good places for short-term money

- **High-yield savings account:** safe, flexible, FDIC insured up to limits.
- **Certificates of deposit (CDs):** a fixed rate for a set term; early withdrawal usually costs a penalty.
- **Money market funds:** low risk and easy access, though not FDIC insured.
- **Treasury bills:** short-term, backed by the U.S. government.

## Tips

- Keep each goal's money separate so you can track progress.
- Automate monthly transfers toward each goal.
- Match CD or Treasury maturities to when you'll need the money.

## The trade-off

These options grow slowly. For goals that are short-term, the priority is having the money when you need it, not maximizing growth.
