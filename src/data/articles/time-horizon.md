# Your Time Horizon

Your time horizon is how long you plan to keep your money invested before you need to use it.

## Why it matters

The longer your time horizon, the more time you have to recover from market drops. That's why time horizon is one of the biggest factors in choosing investments.

- **Short (under about 3 years):** a house down payment next year, an emergency fund. Safety comes first: savings accounts, CDs, money market funds.
- **Medium (about 3 to 10 years):** a mix of stocks and bonds is common.
- **Long (10+ years):** retirement for most young workers. More stocks are common, because there's time to ride out downturns.

## One person, many horizons

You probably have several goals with different horizons. Money for a wedding in two years and money for retirement in 35 years shouldn't be invested the same way.

## As the goal gets closer

Many people gradually move money from stocks toward bonds and cash as a goal approaches. Target date funds do this automatically.
