# What Is a Stock?

A stock (also called a share or equity) is a small piece of ownership in a company. If a company has 1 million shares and you own 1,000, you own 0.1% of it.

## How you can make money

- **Price growth:** if the company does well, its shares may become more valuable, and you can sell for more than you paid.
- **Dividends:** some companies pay part of their profits to shareholders, usually every quarter.

## How you can lose money

If the company struggles, its stock price can fall, sometimes a lot. If a company goes out of business, its stock can become worthless.

## Why stock prices move

Prices change every trading day based on what buyers and sellers think a company is worth: its profits, its future plans, the economy, interest rates and news.

## Stocks in a portfolio

Over long periods, stocks have historically offered higher returns than bonds or savings, but with much bigger ups and downs. Many beginners own stocks through funds, which spread money across many companies at once.
