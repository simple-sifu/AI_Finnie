# Setting Financial Goals

Clear goals make it much easier to decide how much to save and how to invest.

## Make goals specific

Instead of "save more," try:

> "Save $15,000 for a home down payment in 4 years."

A good goal has an **amount**, a **date** and a **purpose**.

## Sort goals by time

- **Short-term (under about 3 years):** emergency fund, vacation, car. Usually best kept in savings.
- **Medium-term (about 3 to 10 years):** home down payment, education. Often a mix of savings and conservative investments.
- **Long-term (10+ years):** retirement. Usually invested for growth.

## Prioritize

Common order:

1. a starter emergency fund,
2. your full employer 401(k) match,
3. paying off high-interest debt,
4. other goals.

## Turn goals into a monthly number

Divide what you need by the months you have, then adjust for expected growth. Finnie's Goals tab does this calculation for you.

## Revisit regularly

Life changes. Check your goals at least once a year.
