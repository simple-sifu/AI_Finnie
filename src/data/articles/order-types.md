# Market Orders and Limit Orders

When you buy or sell a stock or ETF, you choose an order type. The two most common are market orders and limit orders.

## Market order

Buy or sell **right away** at the best available price.

- **Pro:** almost always filled quickly.
- **Con:** you don't control the exact price. In fast-moving markets, the price you get can differ from the last price you saw.

## Limit order

Buy or sell **only at a price you set, or better**.

- A **buy limit** at $50 fills only at $50 or less.
- A **sell limit** at $60 fills only at $60 or more.
- **Pro:** you control the price.
- **Con:** your order may never fill if the price doesn't reach your limit.

## Which to use

- For large, frequently traded stocks and ETFs during normal hours, market orders are usually fine.
- For thinly traded investments, volatile times or trades outside regular hours, limit orders help avoid surprises.

## Other order types

Brokers also offer stop orders and others. Make sure you understand any order type before using it.
