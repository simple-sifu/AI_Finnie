# How Compound Interest Grows Your Money

Compound interest means you earn returns not only on the money you put in, but also on the returns you've already earned. Over time, this "interest on interest" can make a big difference.

## A simple example

Say you invest $1,000 and it grows 7% a year:

- After year 1: $1,070 (you earned $70).
- After year 2: about $1,145 (you earned $75, because the $70 is now earning too).
- After 10 years: about $1,967.
- After 30 years: about $7,612.

You never added more money. The growth came from returns building on returns.

## Why starting early matters

Because compounding builds on itself, time is the most powerful ingredient. Someone who starts investing at 25 can end up with much more than someone who starts at 35, even if the later starter invests more each month.

## Keep in mind

Real investments don't grow by the same amount every year. Some years are up, some are down. The 7% here is just an example to show how compounding works, not a promise of returns.
