# Municipal Bonds

Municipal bonds ("munis") are issued by states, cities, counties and other local governments to pay for things like schools, roads and water systems.

## The main appeal: taxes

Interest on many municipal bonds is exempt from federal income tax. If you buy bonds issued in your own state, the interest may also be exempt from state income tax.

## Who benefits most

The tax break is worth more to people in higher tax brackets. Munis usually pay lower interest than taxable bonds, so for someone in a low bracket, a taxable bond may still come out ahead after tax.

## Types

- **General obligation bonds:** backed by the government's taxing power.
- **Revenue bonds:** repaid from a specific project's income, like a toll road.

## Keep in mind

- Municipal bonds are generally considered fairly safe, but local governments can run into financial trouble.
- Holding munis in a tax-advantaged account like an IRA wastes their tax benefit.
- You can research specific municipal bonds on the MSRB's EMMA website.
