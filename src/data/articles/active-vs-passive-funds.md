# Active vs. Passive Funds

Funds generally follow one of two approaches.

## Active funds

A manager or team picks investments, trying to beat the market.

- **Pros:** the chance to outperform; managers can react to changing conditions.
- **Cons:** higher fees; many active funds trail their benchmark index after fees over long periods.

## Passive (index) funds

The fund simply tracks an index, buying what's in it.

- **Pros:** low fees, broad diversification, predictable results relative to the index.
- **Cons:** you'll never beat the market; you get the market's return, minus a small fee.

## What this means for beginners

Many beginners choose passive index funds because they're simple, cheap and broadly diversified. Some investors mix in active funds for specific goals.

## How to tell them apart

A fund's name and prospectus usually say whether it tracks an index. Active funds typically have noticeably higher expense ratios.
