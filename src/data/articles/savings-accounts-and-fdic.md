# Savings Accounts and FDIC Insurance

A savings account at a bank is one of the safest places to keep money you'll need soon. A big reason is FDIC insurance.

## What the FDIC covers

The FDIC insures deposits at member banks. If an insured bank fails, your deposits are protected up to **$250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, for each account ownership category**.

Covered deposits include:

- checking and savings accounts,
- money market deposit accounts,
- certificates of deposit (CDs).

## What it doesn't cover

Investments are **not** FDIC insured, even if you buy them through a bank:

- stocks, bonds and mutual funds,
- money market **funds**,
- annuities and life insurance,
- crypto assets.

## Earning more on savings

Interest rates vary a lot between banks. Some online banks offer "high-yield" savings accounts with much higher rates than traditional ones. Compare rates, and confirm the bank is FDIC insured.

## Savings vs. investing

Savings accounts are for safety and easy access. Over long periods, their interest may not keep up with inflation, which is why long-term goals usually call for investing.
