# Dividends

A dividend is a payment a company makes to its shareholders, usually from its profits. It's a way for a company to share its earnings with the people who own it.

## How dividends work

- Dividends are usually paid every quarter, in cash.
- They're paid per share. If a company pays $0.50 per share each quarter and you own 100 shares, you get $50 each quarter.
- Not all companies pay dividends. Younger, fast-growing companies often reinvest their profits instead.

## Dividend yield

Dividend yield compares the yearly dividend to the stock price. A stock priced at $100 that pays $3 a year has a 3% yield.

## Reinvesting dividends

Many brokerages let you automatically reinvest dividends to buy more shares. This helps your investment compound over time.

## Keep in mind

- Companies can cut or stop dividends at any time.
- A very high yield can be a warning sign: sometimes it's high because the stock price has fallen sharply.
- In a taxable account, dividends are usually taxed in the year you receive them.
