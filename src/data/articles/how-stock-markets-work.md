# How the Stock Market Works

The stock market is where buyers and sellers trade shares of public companies.

## Exchanges

Most U.S. stock trading happens on exchanges like the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) and Nasdaq. Regular trading hours are 9:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. Eastern time on weekdays, except market holidays.

## How you buy and sell

You don't trade on an exchange directly. You place orders through a **brokerage account**, and your broker sends the order to be filled.

## What sets prices

Prices are set by supply and demand. If more people want to buy a stock than sell it, the price tends to rise; if more want to sell, it tends to fall. Company results, economic news and interest rates all shape what investors are willing to pay.

## The role of the SEC

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission oversees the securities markets to protect investors, for example by requiring public companies to share financial information.

## Keep in mind

Daily price moves are often noise. For long-term investors, a company's or the overall market's long-term direction matters more than any single day.
