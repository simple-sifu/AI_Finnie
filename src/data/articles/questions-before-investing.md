# Five Questions to Ask Before You Invest

The SEC suggests asking a few basic questions before putting money into any investment.

## 1. Is the seller licensed?

Check that anyone selling you an investment is registered. You can look up brokers and advisers for free using the SEC's and FINRA's search tools.

## 2. Is the investment registered?

Many investment offerings must be registered with the SEC. Unregistered investments carry extra risk and are common in scams.

## 3. How do the risks compare with the potential rewards?

Every investment has risk. If someone promises high returns with little or no risk, be very careful: that's a classic warning sign of fraud.

## 4. Do I understand the investment?

If you can't explain in your own words what you're buying and how it makes money, wait until you can.

## 5. Where can I turn for help?

Know who to contact if something goes wrong, including the SEC and your state securities regulator.

## The bottom line

Taking a few minutes to ask these questions can protect you from costly mistakes and outright fraud.
